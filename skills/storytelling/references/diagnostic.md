# Story check: what to look for

Rate each item Strong, Thin or Missing. Quote the draft when you explain a rating.

| # | Check | Strong | Thin | Missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Want** | A specific, visible goal ("get 40 clips into a cozy 30s reel by morning") | A vague aim ("make a good video") | No goal; just things that happened |
| 2 | **Obstacle** | Something resists: skill, time, people, tools, fear, or the character's own habit | Difficulty is named but not shown ("it was hard") | Everything goes smoothly |
| 3 | **Stakes** | Something real can be lost or won, and the viewer can feel it | Stakes exist but are abstract or low | Nothing depends on the outcome |
| 4 | **Gap** | The plan visibly fails or surprises, forcing a new move | A small surprise with no consequence | Outcome exactly as expected |
| 5 | **Change** | A clear before and after, shown at one moment (a realization or decision) | Change is announced ("I learned that...") | No change; the ending equals the beginning |
| 6 | **Chain** | Beats link with "but" and "therefore" | Mostly "and then" with a few links | A list of events or facts |
| 7 | **Specifics** | One vivid scene with concrete details | Some details, mostly summary | Abstract or generic language throughout |
| 8 | **Loop and payoff** | A question is opened early and answered at the end | A promise with a weak or generic payoff | No question, or a hook the video never delivers |

## Gates

- **One idea?** If the draft holds two takeaways, pick one and park the other as a separate video.
- **Viewer first?** Would a stranger care in the first seconds? Cut material that is interesting to the creator but not to the audience.

## Quick tests

- **And / But / Therefore.** Summarize the draft in three sentences: "X happens, AND ..., BUT ..., THEREFORE ...". If you cannot write the "but", there is no conflict yet. If every link is "and then", it is a list.
- **So what?** After each beat, ask what changed. If nothing, the beat is a candidate to cut.
- **Trying versus winning.** Does the viewer see effort and a setback, or only the result?
- **Opposite ends.** Is the end state meaningfully different from the start? If not, there is no change.
- **Replace the abstraction.** Find every vague word (amazing, difficult, stressful, great) and ask what that looked like in a specific moment.

## Flatness patterns (red flags)

- Opens with a thesis, background or "today I want to talk about"
- Chronological summary with no turning point
- Success without struggle ("I did X and it worked great")
- The moral announced rather than shown
- No reaction or emotion from the person at the centre
- Stakes mentioned only in general terms
- Generic call to action as the whole ending
- Several ideas competing for the same 30 seconds
- A hook that promises something the video does not give

## Severity

- **Fix first:** missing want, obstacle, stakes or change. These make the draft a report.
- **Fix next:** weak gap, chain and payoff. These flatten pacing and retention.
- **Polish last:** specifics, wording and hook phrasing.
