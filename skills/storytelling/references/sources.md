# Sources

Research for this skill was collected on 2026-10-18 from the web. Summaries in `principles.md` are paraphrases; read the originals for depth. Where a source is a blog summary of a book, treat it as secondary.

## Craft

- **Robert McKee, *Story* (1997).** The expectation gap, want versus need, conflict as the engine of change.
  - https://graham-edwards.com/2015/09/16/what-is-story/
  - https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/22839401-the-gap-in-stories-la-robert-mckee
  - https://writershelpingwriters.net/2019/11/surprising-your-readers-in-every-scene/
  - https://katrinschumann.substack.com/p/anatomy-of-a-novel-21
- **Lisa Cron, *Wired for Story* (2012) and *Story Genius* (2016).** Story as inner change under pressure, the misbelief, specifics.
  - https://www.thecreativepenn.com/2016/11/07/story-genius-lisa-cron
  - https://writerunboxed.com/2021/03/08/still-crazy-after-all-these-years/
  - https://thegoodmancenter.com/blog/icymi-why-we-are-wired-for-story
- **Emma Coats, Pixar's 22 rules of storytelling (2011).**
  - https://laughingsquid.com/22-rules-of-storytelling/
  - https://gizmodo.com/the-22-rules-of-storytelling-according-to-pixar-5916970
- **Dan Harmon's Story Circle.**
  - https://kindlepreneur.com/dan-harmon-story-circle/
  - https://dabblewriter.com/the-story-circle
- **Randy Olson, *Houston, We Have a Narrative* (2015), and Trey Parker and Matt Stone's rule of replacing "and then".**
  - https://ncse.ngo/and-therefore-randy-olson-and-art-science-storytelling-part-1
  - https://businessofstory.com/cartmans-storytelling-secret-that-every-cmo-should-steal/
- **Matthew Dicks, *Storyworthy* (2018).** Five-second moment, stakes, starting with the opposite.
  - https://www.supersummary.com/storyworthy/summary/
  - https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-tell-better-stories-matthew
- **Will Storr, *The Science of Storytelling*.** Character, flaw and unexpected change.
  - https://humanjourney.us/library-book/the-science-of-storytelling
- **Kindra Hall, *Stories That Stick* (2019).** Four elements and Normal, Explosion, New Normal.
  - https://www.prezent.ai/blog/storytelling-in-business-kindra-hall
  - https://learningleader.com/episode/329-kindra-hall-how-storytelling-can-influence-audiences-transform-your-business/
- **Kurt Vonnegut, the shapes of stories.**
  - https://fs.blog/kurt-vonnegut-the-shapes-of-stories/
  - https://www.mentalfloss.com/literature/authors/kurt-vonneguts-story-diagrams

  - https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/nova/article/how-to-stimulate-curiosity/
- **Melanie Green and Timothy Brock (2000, 2002), narrative transportation.**
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transportation_theory_(psychology)
  - https://mcm.uni-wuerzburg.de/fileadmin/06110300/2024/Pdfs/Green___Appel__2024__Advances_Preprint.pdf
  - https://thejuryexpert.com/2011/05/narrative-persuasion/

McKee's *Story*, Cron's *Wired for Story*, Storr's *The Science of Storytelling*, Dicks's *Storyworthy*, Hall's *Stories That Stick*, Olson's *Houston, We Have a Narrative*, and Loewenstein's 1994 paper.
