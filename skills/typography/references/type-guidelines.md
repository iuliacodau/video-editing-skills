# Type guidelines for 9:16 video

## Safe zones (approximate, verify before publishing)

Platform apps overlay buttons, usernames and captions on videos, and the layout changes over time. As a cautious default on a 1080x1920 canvas:

- Keep important text roughly **150-250 px away from the top** edge.
- Keep it roughly **300-450 px away from the bottom** edge (the caption and profile area).
- Keep it roughly **100-150 px away from the right** edge (like, comment and share buttons).
- Check each platform's current published guidance or preview in the app before finalizing.

## Size starting points (1080x1920)

| Role | Starting size | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook / headline | 80-140 px | Bold or heavy weight, 1-6 words |
| Supporting line | 50-70 px | Medium or regular, high contrast |
| Label / small text | 40-50 px | Only for non-essential info |

## Pairing approach

- **One family, multiple weights** is the safest option (for example heavy for headline, regular for support).
- **Contrast pairing:** a bold sans-serif display with a clean sans-serif text face; or a serif display with a sans-serif text face for an editorial feel.
- Avoid pairing two similar-but-different fonts; the difference reads as a mistake.

## Style by tone

| Tone | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Energetic / youth | Heavy geometric or grotesque sans, tight and bold |
| Editorial / premium | High-contrast serif headlines, light sans for support |
| Friendly / lifestyle | Rounded or humanist sans |
| Technical / tutorial | Neutral sans or monospace for code and labels |
| Playful | Hand-drawn or display face for a few words only |

## Readability checklist

- [ ] Readable at arm's length on a phone, with the screen dimmed
- [ ] Contrast at least about 4.5:1 for normal text (aim for more on moving video)
- [ ] Shadow, outline or background box if the video behind is busy
- [ ] No text over faces or key action
- [ ] Line length short; break lines at natural phrases
- [ ] Letter spacing slightly looser for all-caps text
- [ ] Consistent margins and alignment

## Text timing

- Allow roughly 0.3 seconds per word for short cards, with a minimum of about 1 second, then test by reading it aloud once at normal speed.
- Text that appears with a spoken line should appear just before or as the word is said.
- Use the same in and out animation style across the video (cut, fade, slide, pop) for a coherent look.

## Fonts: where to find open-license options

- Google Fonts (most are under the SIL Open Font License)
- Fontshare and other free foundries (check each font's license)
- Always read the license for commercial or client work.
