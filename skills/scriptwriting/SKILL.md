# Scriptwriting for short-form video

Write scripts to be **heard and seen**, not read. Output is a beat-by-beat script the user can film or record from.

## Process

1. **Get the inputs.** A story brief if one exists (otherwise ask for idea, platform, length, audience, tone), plus the speaker: on camera, voiceover, or text only.
2. **Set the word budget.** Spoken pace is roughly 2.5-3 words per second, so 30s is about 75-90 words and 60s is about 150-180 words. Leave room for pauses and visual-only moments. Adjust if the user speaks slower or faster.
3. **Draft the hook.** One or two short sentences. No greeting, no "hey guys", no throat-clearing.
4. **Write beat by beat.** One idea per beat. Short sentences. Spoken contractions. Concrete words.
5. **Add visuals and on-screen text** for every beat (see format below). If a beat has no visual, add one or cut the beat.
6. **Read-aloud pass.** Cut anything that is awkward to say, remove words that repeat the on-screen text exactly, and trim to the word budget.
7. **End cleanly.** Close the hook's loop, then one call to action at most.

## Output format

Use a table so it can be filmed and edited from directly:

| # | Time | Voiceover / spoken line | Visual / shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-3s | ... | ... | ... |

Then add: **Word count**, **Estimated runtime**, and **Notes** (b-roll to capture, sound or music cue, anything to verify).

## Writing rules

- Write the way the speaker talks. If they would not say it out loud, rewrite it.
- Front-load the point of every sentence.
- Prefer verbs over adjectives. Cut "really", "very", "just", "basically".
- On-screen text should add or emphasize, not transcribe. Use 1-6 words per card.
- One call to action, phrased as one specific action.
- Do not invent facts, statistics or claims. Mark anything unverified as `[verify]`.

## Variants

When asked, produce alternate hooks, a shorter cut (for example 60s down to 30s by removing a whole beat, not by speeding up), or a version for another platform. See `references/script-patterns.md` for hook lines and a blank template.
