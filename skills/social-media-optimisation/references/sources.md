## Known conflicts to resolve in the app

- Carousel limit: 20 (announcement) vs 10 (cached Help Center page, API schedulers).
- Reels maximum: 20 minutes (Instagram Reels page) vs 3 minutes (earlier statements, reach limit) vs 15 minutes (some upload paths).
- Hashtag limit: 5 (announced, gradual rollout) vs 30 (older accounts, older guides).
- Grid crop rollout date: January 2025 vs late 2025.
- Reel cover size: 420 x 654 (cited Help Center) vs 1080 x 1920 (many guides).
- Caption cutoff: 125 characters (single-sourced).
