# Instagram Reels

**Last verified: 2026-10-18.** Shared rules (eligibility, originality, ranking, caption, hashtags, grid) are in `shared.md`. Confidence labels are explained there.

## Blockers (fix first)

- [ ] **3 minutes or less** to be recommended to non-followers. Longer Reels can still be posted for followers. (Official)
- [ ] **Full-screen vertical 9:16, high resolution, no borders.** (Official)
- [ ] **No watermarks**, including ones from other apps. (Official)
- [ ] **Text does not cover most of the screen.** (Official)
- [ ] **Has audio.** Muted reels are listed among those made less visible. (Official, 2023)
- [ ] **Not already posted on Instagram.** Reused content needs material changes such as a new voiceover, commentary or real edits. (Official)
- [ ] **Not blurry or low quality.** (Official)

## Specs

- Aspect ratio: 9:16, 1080 x 1920 recommended. Instagram accepts ratios between 1.91:1 and 9:16, but anything other than 9:16 risks borders. (Reported)
- Minimum quality: 720p and 30 fps. (Reported, citing the Help Center)
- Length: recording or uploading up to 20 minutes is possible. (Official, Instagram Reels page) Keep to 3 minutes or less for reach. Upload paths differ (some tools cap at 15 minutes), so check the app. (Reported)
- Cover: the cover cannot be edited after posting, so choose it before sharing. (Reported, citing the Help Center) The Help Center's recommended cover size is 420 x 654 (1:1.55) per secondary reports, while many guides say full 9:16. (Conflicting, Unverified) Design on 9:16 and keep key content central (see below).

## First seconds

- Instagram's creator guidance: pull the viewer in within the first 3 seconds and keep them interested the whole time. (Official)
- [ ] The first frame is a strong image, not black, a logo or a slow title.
- [ ] The hook (line or on-screen text) lands immediately. Hook content comes from the real story (`storytelling`).
- [ ] Audio starts on frame one.
- [ ] No greeting or throat-clearing (`scriptwriting`).

## Watch-through and ending

- Instagram names watching all the way through, resharing, liking and visiting the audio page as the most important Reels predictions. (Official, 2023)
- [ ] The Reel is as short as the story allows. Cut the dead tail.
- [ ] It ends on the payoff, with one call to action at most.
- [ ] Momentum holds mid-video: something new (information, angle, graphic) where interest could dip (`storytelling`).
- A seamless loop (last frame flows into the first) is a common practitioner idea. Its effect on ranking is Unverified.

## Sound-off and small screen

- [ ] Key points work with sound off (`captions`, on-screen text), without text covering most of the screen. (Official)
- [ ] Key content avoids the top, bottom and right edges where the app overlays buttons and text. Instagram's exact overlay positions were not documented in the sources I found (Unverified); check the in-app preview. See `typography` for general guidance.

## Cover and profile grid

- Profile tiles are 3:4 and cropped from the centre. From a 1080 x 1920 cover, a centred 3:4 crop keeps 1080 x 1440, trimming about 240 px (12.5%) from the top and from the bottom. (Derived from the 3:4 grid, Reported)
- [ ] Cover text, faces and logos sit in the middle of the frame, roughly the middle 75% of the height.
- [ ] Cover text is a label of a few words, not a caption. Use the same type and palette as the rest of the profile (`typography`, `color-palettes`).
- [ ] Preview the tile on the profile; use Adjust preview if it clips.

## Caption and hashtags

- [ ] First line carries the hook or main keyword (the visible cutoff is Unverified; see `shared.md`).
- [ ] Keywords are in the caption, not the comments.
- [ ] No more than 5 hashtags, specific to the content.

## Trial Reels (optional)

- A Trial Reel is shown only to non-followers first, so a hook can be tested without followers seeing it. (Reported)
- Eligibility thresholds differ across sources (200 or 1,000 followers; account type). (Unverified) Check in the app.
- Test one variable at a time (hook, length, audio) so the result means something.
