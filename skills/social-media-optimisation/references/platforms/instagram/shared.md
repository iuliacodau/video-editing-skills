# Instagram: rules that apply to every format

**Last verified: 2026-10-18** (web research in this session). Instagram changes things often; re-verify anything marked Reported or Unverified, and anything older than about 90 days.

**Labels:** **Official** = read on an Instagram or Meta page. **Official reported** = an Instagram statement relayed by news sources. **Reported** = several third-party sources agree. **Unverified** = single or conflicting sources.

## Recommendation eligibility

- Instagram recommends content only from **public accounts**; Creator and Business accounts are public automatically. (Official)
- Eligible content is original, does not violate Community Guidelines, has **no visible watermarks**, and meets the Recommendations Guidelines. (Official, 2024)
- Allowed but possibly not recommended: clickbait or engagement bait, unoriginal content largely repurposed from other sources, exaggerated health or finance claims, political content, content that may depict violence or be sexually suggestive. See the full Recommendations Guidelines. (Official, 2024)
- Eligibility does not guarantee recommendation. (Official, Help Center)
- Professional accounts can check eligibility in Settings, Account, Account Status. (Official)

## Originality

- When identical content appears more than once, Instagram says it recommends only the original, and may label copies with a link to the original creator. (Official, 2024)
- Accounts that repeatedly post others' content without creating or enhancing it in a material way may not be shown in recommendations. (Official, 2024)
- In April 2026 this was extended to photos and carousels: accounts that mainly post unoriginal reels, photos or carousels may not be seen in recommendations to new audiences, and low-effort edits such as watermarks or credits do not count as original. (Official reported, Engadget, 2026-04-30)
- Practical rule: use your own footage and images. If you use someone else's material, add real commentary, narration or graphics.

## Ranking: what Instagram says it looks at

- Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels each use their own ranking. (Official, 2023-05-31)
- **Reels:** the most important predictions are how likely someone is to reshare, watch all the way through, like, and visit the audio page. (Official, 2023)
- **Feed:** the interactions looked at most closely are spending a few seconds on a post, commenting, liking, sharing and tapping the profile photo. Popularity signals (how many and how quickly people like, comment, share and save) also matter. (Official, 2023)
- **Explore:** predictions centre on likes, saves and shares; popularity signals matter more than in Feed. (Official, 2023)
- Instagram's head said in January 2025 that watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach matter most. (Reported; I did not find the primary post.) Claims that sends weigh several times more than likes are **Unverified**; do not quote them.

## Captions and search

- Caption limit is 2,200 characters. (Reported)
- Roughly the first 125 characters show before "more". (Unverified; check in the app preview.)
- For Search, put keywords and hashtags **in the caption, not the comments**, and use relevant keywords in the handle and bio. (Official, creators blog 2022, updated 2023)

## Hashtags

- In December 2025 Instagram's @creators account said it would gradually reduce the hashtags allowed in a reel or post caption to **5**. (Official reported, Social Media Today and others)
- The rollout is gradual, so some accounts may still see the old limit of 30. (Unverified)
- Use a few specific tags that match the content. Hashtags help Instagram understand a post; they are not a reach switch. (Reported)

## Profile grid

- The profile grid shows **3:4 tiles** instead of squares, cropped from the centre of the image or cover. Instagram's head announced the vertical grid in January 2025. (Official reported; some sources date the rollout to late 2025, Unverified timing.)
- A tile can usually be repositioned with "Adjust preview" on the post. (Reported)
- Always view the profile on a phone after posting.

## Calls to action

- Ask for one specific thing that fits the content. Avoid engagement bait (for example "comment X to unlock", tag chains, begging for likes).

## Not researched

Posting times and frequency, Stories, Insights metrics, collabs, paid promotion, shopping tags, alt text. Do not give advice on these from this file.
