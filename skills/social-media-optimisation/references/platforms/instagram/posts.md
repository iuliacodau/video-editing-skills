# Instagram feed posts (single image)

**Last verified: 2026-10-18.** Shared rules are in `shared.md`. A single video shared to Instagram is generally published as a Reel (Reported), so use `reels.md` for video.

## Blockers (fix first)

- [ ] **Original image or graphic.** Instagram extended its originality rules to photos in April 2026; watermarks or credits on someone else's image do not make it original. (Official reported)
- [ ] **No visible watermarks** from other apps or sites. (Official for eligibility)
- [ ] **Public account** if you want recommendations. (Official)

## Format and crop

- Instagram lets you pick square, portrait or landscape for a post. (Official, Help Center) Portrait 4:5 is the common choice for taking the most feed space. Ratios of 1:1 and 1.91:1 are also supported. (Reported)
- The profile tile is a 3:4 centre crop. For a 1080 x 1350 (4:5) image, the reported 1013 x 1350 tile trims about 33 px from each side. (Reported)
- [ ] The subject and any text sit in the centre, clear of the edges.
- [ ] The image is checked in the profile tile preview, not just the post.

## What the image has to do

- Instagram says Feed ranking looks closely at whether people spend a few seconds on a post, comment, like, share or tap the profile photo. (Official, 2023)
- [ ] One clear focal point that reads at tile size on a phone.
- [ ] If there is text on the image, it is short, large and high contrast (`typography`, `color-palettes`).
- [ ] The image makes someone pause. If it is a statement graphic, the statement is specific (`storytelling`).
- Whether text-heavy photos are limited in recommendations is only documented for Reels (Official). For posts it is Unverified.

## Caption and hashtags

- [ ] The first line carries the point or keyword.
- [ ] Keywords are in the caption, not the comments. (Official)
- [ ] No more than 5 hashtags, specific to the content.
- [ ] One specific call to action at most, no engagement bait.
