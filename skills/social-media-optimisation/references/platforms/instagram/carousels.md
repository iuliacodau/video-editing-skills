# Instagram carousels

**Last verified: 2026-10-18.** Shared rules are in `shared.md`.

## Blockers (fix first)

- [ ] **Original content.** The April 2026 originality rules explicitly cover carousels. (Official reported)
- [ ] **One orientation for every slide.** Instagram applies the orientation you choose (square, portrait or landscape) to the whole post; you cannot set it per item. (Official, Help Center)
- [ ] **Slide count within the limit.** Instagram announced 20 photos and videos per carousel in August 2024. (Official reported) The Help Center page I saw still said 10, which appears outdated, and some scheduling tools stay at 10 because of API limits. (Reported) Check in the app and in the tool you post with.

## Structure

- The first photo or video of the carousel is what shows in the feed and on the profile grid. (Official, Help Center)
- [ ] **Slide 1 is the hook and the cover.** It works alone, in the 3:4 profile tile centre crop, and gives a reason to swipe. Design it with key content in the middle.
- [ ] **Each slide has one job** and one idea (`storytelling`: each slide is a beat).
- [ ] **Every slide gives a reason to swipe to the next**: an open question, a step still missing, a result held back.
- [ ] **The last slide delivers the payoff** and holds at most one specific call to action.
- [ ] **Slide count follows the story.** 20 is a ceiling, not a target. (Practice, not a platform rule)
- [ ] **Consistent system across slides:** same margins, type and palette (`typography`, `color-palettes`).

## Mechanics to know

- One caption and one location apply to the whole post; people can be tagged on individual photos. (Official, Help Center)
- Likes and comments belong to the whole post, not to individual slides. (Official, Help Center)
- Videos inside a carousel are **not Reels**: they do not appear in the Reels tab or get Reels features. (Official, Help Center)
- Slides cannot be added after sharing, but the post can be deleted. (Reported, quoting the Help Center)
- Maximum length of a video slide (60 seconds in some sources) is Unverified.

## Caption and hashtags

- [ ] The caption carries the whole post's point and keywords, since there is no per-slide caption. (Official: one caption per post)
- [ ] Keywords are in the caption, not the comments. (Official)
- [ ] No more than 5 hashtags, specific to the content.

## Text on slides

- [ ] Large, short text, readable on a phone at arm's length (`typography`).
- [ ] High contrast against the slide background (`color-palettes`).
- [ ] Not so dense that it needs zooming in. If a slide needs more than a few lines, split it.
